    Currency Converter

    A real-time financial tool that calculates the exchange value between two international currencies based on current market rates.

    History

    Exchange began with barter systems (3000 BCE). The 1944 Bretton Woods Accord established fixed rates against the US Dollar and gold, evolving into the modern free-floating market in the 1970s.

    How to Use

    • Select your local currency and the target foreign currency.
    • Enter the amount to convert.
    • View the result based on mid-market exchange rates.
    Pro Tip Section

    🚀 Pro Tip:

    "Remember that market rates are 'mid-market'—banks and exchange bureaus often apply a spread or fee on top of these numbers."
