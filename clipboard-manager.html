How to Use Clipboard Manager

  1. Add a clip: Type or paste your text, then click Add Clip.
  2. Copy a clip: Click the Copy button next to any clip to copy instantly.
  3. Remove/Pin: Use Remove or Pin to manage your clips.
  4. Clips are saved: All your clips are stored in your browser and remain after reload.
