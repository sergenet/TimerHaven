Clipboard Manager

About the Clipboard Manager

The Clipboard Manager is a lightweight, privacy‑first tool for storing and reusing text snippets. It’s designed for quick repetition tasks — email signatures, short code fragments, addresses, and commonly used phrases — without installing extra software. Everything you save here is stored in your browser (localStorage) so you remain in control of your data.

How it helps

Instead of searching through documents or typing the same content repeatedly, keep frequent snippets ready with a single click. Use pinning to keep important items at the top, and the built‑in copy action to instantly move text to your system clipboard.

Quick usage

  1. Paste or type text into the input field and click Add Clip.
  2. Click the Copy button next to any clip to copy it to your device clipboard.
  3. Click Pin to keep a clip at the top, or Remove to delete it.

Practical tips

  • Keep clips short and descriptive — that makes finding the right snippet quicker.
  • Create categories mentally (e.g., Signatures, Code, Addresses) and prefix items (SIG:, CODE:) for easy scanning.
  • Avoid storing passwords or highly sensitive data here; use a secure password manager instead.

FAQ

Is my data uploaded to your servers?
No. By default clips are saved locally in your browser and are not transmitted to the server unless you explicitly export or copy them elsewhere.
Will clips persist across browsers or devices?
Not automatically. Clips persist in the same browser on the same device. To move clips between devices, export and import, or use a sync feature if you set one up externally.
FAQs
  • Are my notes saved? Yes! Notes are saved in your browser and remain until you clear them.

Clipboard Tips

  • Keep clips short: Easier to scan and reuse.
  • Pin important: Keep frequent items on top.
  • Remove old: Clean up outdated clips regularly.
