Secure Password Generator

A security tool that creates high-entropy, unpredictable passwords to protect your digital accounts from unauthorized access.

History

Modern computer passwords originated at MIT in 1960. Entropy, the measure of a password's randomness, is the key to defeating brute-force attacks.

How to Use

  • Choose your desired length (12+ characters recommended).
  • Include symbols, numbers, and mixed-case letters.
  • Generate and copy your secure password.
Pro Tip Section

🚀 Pro Tip:
