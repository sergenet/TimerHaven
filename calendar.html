        Online Calendar

        A digital calendar tool for organizing dates, events, and schedules. It builds on thousands of years of human timekeeping evolution.

        History

        The earliest calendars were lunar-based (Babylonian/Egyptian). In 46 BC, Julius Caesar introduced the Julian calendar, which was refined in 1582 by Pope Gregory XIII into the Gregorian system used today.

        How to Use

        • Navigate to the current month or search for a specific date.
        • Add events or notes to specific days.
        • Use the 'Today' button to quickly return to the current date.
