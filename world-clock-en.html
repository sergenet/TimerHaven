World Clock & Time Zones

A World Clock displays the current time for various locations around the globe, accounting for the complex web of international time zones and daylight saving adjustments.

History

Standard time was popularized by railroads in the 19th century. Sir Sandford Fleming proposed the 24-zone system in 1878, and the Greenwich Meridian was established as 'longitude zero' at the 1884 International Prime Meridian Conference.

How to Use

  • Select the city or country you wish to track.
  • The tool calculates the offset from Coordinated Universal Time (UTC).
  • Check the 'Daylight Saving Time' indicator for accurate local time.
