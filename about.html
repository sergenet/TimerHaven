About TimerHaven

TimerHaven provides a suite of lightweight, privacy-friendly web tools to help you manage time, tasks, and small workflows — timers, clocks, a clipboard manager, converters and more. All tools are free to use and require no sign‑in.

The site is maintained by an individual developer and updated regularly. Our focus is speed, privacy, and simplicity so you can get things done without installing extra software.

Source & feedback
