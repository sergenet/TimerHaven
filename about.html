Meet the Creator: Serge Shammas

TimerHaven was founded by Serge Shammas, a seasoned productivity writer and User Experience (UX) researcher with over a decade of experience in optimizing digital workflows. Throughout his career, Serge has observed a recurring problem in the productivity space: tools are either too simple to be effective or too complex for daily use.

With a background in human-computer interaction and a passion for time-management science, Serge built TimerHaven to provide "just enough" functionality to support deep work, effective scheduling, and accurate time tracking.

The TimerHaven Philosophy

Every tool on this site is built with three core principles in mind:

  • Privacy First: Most productivity apps profit from your data. TimerHaven uses local storage whenever possible, meaning your notes, settings, and timers stay on your device, not our servers.
  • Utility Without Friction: We believe you shouldn't have to "learn" a tool to start being productive. Our interfaces are zero-fuss and optimized for immediate action.
  • Methodology-Led Design: We don't just build timers; we build systems. Whether it's the Pomodoro Technique, Eisenhower Matrix, or GTD, our tools are designed to fit into proven productivity frameworks.

Our Methodology

Unlike generic utility sites, TimerHaven provides comprehensive Actionable Guides for every tool. We believe that a tool is only as good as the habit it supports. That's why we spend as much time researching and writing our productivity articles as we do coding the applications.

Our content is based on:

  • Scientific research into cognitive focus and recovery.
  • Real-world case studies from developers, students, and professionals.
  • Continuous testing and user feedback from our global community.

Source & Transparency

Transparency is the foundation of trust. TimerHaven is an open project, and we welcome contributions from the community. You can find our source code, report bugs, or suggest new features on our GitHub repository: sergenet/TimerHaven.
