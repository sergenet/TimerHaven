How to Use Notes

The Notes tool is designed for quick ideas, lists, and reminders. Here’s how to use it:

Advertisement

Step-by-step Guide

  1. Add a note: Type your text in the input box.
  2. Save the note: Click Add Note to add it to your list.
  3. View notes: All notes appear in the list below the form.
  4. Delete notes: Remove notes you no longer need.

FAQs

  • Are notes saved after refresh? Yes, until you clear your browser storage.
  • Can I add multiple notes? Yes, as many as you wish.
  • Can I edit a note? Not yet—delete and add a new note to update.

Tips for Success

  • Use short notes for quick ideas or tasks.
  • Delete old notes to stay organized.
  • Use notes for reminders, lists, or important points.
