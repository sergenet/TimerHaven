Quick notes that stay with you — Use Notes for short reminders, ideas or session logs. Notes are saved locally in your browser so they remain private to your device unless you export them. Pin important items to keep them at the top.

By Serge Shammas · — Last updated: Jan 8, 2026

FAQs
  • Are notes saved? Yes, in your browser until you clear them.
  • Can I pin important notes? Yes — pin/unpin moves notes to the top.
  • Is there a limit? No, add as many notes as you need.
