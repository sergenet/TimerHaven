Free Online Stopwatch with Laps

A stopwatch is a precision timekeeper designed to measure the exact amount of time elapsed between activation and deactivation. Whether you're tracking your workout intervals, timing a presentation, or conducting a science experiment, our free online stopwatch provides accuracy down to the millisecond with an easy-to-use lap feature.

Why Use a Digital Stopwatch?

While physical stopwatches were once the standard for coaches and scientists, digital online stopwatches offer unmatched convenience. You can access it instantly from any browser on your desktop or mobile device. There are no batteries to replace, and features like unlimited lap tracking allow you to monitor complex, multi-stage events with ease.

How to Use the Lap Feature

  • Start: Click the Start button to begin the overall timer.
  • Record a Lap: Click the 'Lap' button every time a specific interval finishes (e.g., finishing one lap on a track). This records the split time in a list below without pausing the main timer.
  • Review: Analyze your lap times to find your fastest and slowest intervals.
  • Stop and Reset: Use Stop to freeze the clock, and Reset to clear all data and start fresh.

Common Uses for an Online Stopwatch

  • Fitness and Workouts: Time your HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training) rest periods, sprints, or plank holds.
  • Cooking and Baking: Keep track of exactly how long a dish has been in the oven or on the stove.
  • Work and Productivity: Time how long it takes you to write an email, complete a coding task, or read a document to improve your time estimation skills.
  • Public Speaking: Practice your presentation pacing by timing individual slides using the lap function.
